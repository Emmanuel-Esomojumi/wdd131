About Zone

A student-built showcase for robotics kits and tech gadgets.

Our Story

Zone began with a simple goal: help grow Nigeria's tech industry — and Africa's — from the ground up. We wanted to make tech components, tools, materials, accessories, and gadgets more readily available to anyone who wants to build, learn, or experiment.

We're committed to serving tech enthusiasts, students, hobbyists, and innovators with the best products we can find, at prices that don't put quality tech out of reach. Zone is meant to be a hub — a place where anyone with a passion for tech can find materials, pick up insights, and buy at a fair price.

Our Impact

💰

Economic Impact

Affordable access to components lowers the barrier for students and small builders to start real income-generating projects.

🎓

Education

Beginner-to-advanced categories double as a learning path, helping students build real skills with real hardware.

🏭

Industry

Every kit sold is a small step toward a stronger local tech ecosystem — more builders today, more engineers and founders tomorrow.

🏗️

Infrastructure

Reliable access to sensors, boards, and connectivity hardware supports the backbone behind smart homes, automation, and IoT.

💡

Innovation

Putting components directly in the hands of curious builders helps fuel the next wave of homegrown African tech innovation.

What We Stand For

⭐

Quality

Every product listed is based on real, well-reviewed kits and gadgets, not made-up filler.

📘

Learning First
